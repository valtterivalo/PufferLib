## Combat styles used

- The wiki: "Due to Sol Heredit's incredibly high ranged and magic defence, a strong slashing melee weapon (i.e., abyssal tentacle or better) is essential for completing the Colosseum. However, the waves can effectively be cleared with strong ranged or magic setups, and at least one of these options is highly recommended for learners to simplify waves." [1]
- "A twisted bow setup is the strongest option overall for a first quiver; a Tumeken's shadow setup provides significant QoL by one-shotting the melee frem, but suffers later into the wave from lower damage output against the other NPCs." Even with a Scythe of vitur, "a melee-only setup is extremely difficult to use on a first quiver" and "not bringing some kind of ranged option will make your first quiver much harder to obtain." [1]
- So the beginner shape is ranged-primary (or budget melee-primary with a real ranged setup), with melee carried for Sol Heredit, not melee-primary with a token ranged switch. The Budget tab inverts it: "In a budget setup, your best DPS will come from your melee setup. You should try to melee where possible, but make sure to use the ranged setup for tough situations." [1]
- Sun fish's three "essential ingredients": a proper ranged DPS setup for the waves, weapons for the Fremennik warband trio (one-shot/two-shot tools), and a slash weapon for the boss [3].
- Kaoz: "melee and ranged is your best bet", magic damage is poor without a Tumeken's shadow [4].
- Spellbook: Arceuus for Death Charge (thralls optional) is the default recommendation. Ancients only if using a Shadow or as an ironman missing healing items [1][3].
- Suggested minimum stats (wiki): Attack 95+, Strength 95+, Defence 95+, Prayer 85+, Ranged 95+ (97+ "very helpful" to one-shot the Fremennik warband seer with venator bow, dragon arrows, Rigour), Magic 94+ (99 helpful to one-shot the berserker with Tumeken's shadow, occult necklace, tormented bracelet, one piece of Ancestral robes), Hitpoints 95+ [1]. Kaoz: "around the base 80s or 85s" workable with his items, "minimum 95s or simply maxed" for a realistic chance [4].

## Spec weapon

- Tier 1 in every beginner-relevant wiki tab is Saradomin godsword + Dragon claws together [1].
- Saradomin godsword: "very useful to have to one-shot the ranged frem with minimal switches, and the special attack can restore large amounts of health and prayer to stabilise from a rough wave start, or to heal from mistakes later during the wave" [1]. Sun fish: "For the range frem, a Saradomin godsword is by far the best option... As a learner, you'll probably be speccing the frem most of the time." [3] tonsofxp echoes "SGS spec for Range" frem [6].
- Dragon claws / burning claws: "Dumping dragon claws/burning claws special attacks is a very effective way to finish the Sol fight once he goes below 10% HP. Claws are also very useful for finishing NPCs before reinforcements spawn. For a first quiver, dragon claws are easier to use than burning claws, due to the higher burst of damage." [1] Sun fish: "A spec weapon is not a must-have, but it's very nice for killing something early with double south, nuking the shaman when you get mage first reinforcements, and speccing the boss out in enrage phase. Dragon claws are best, followed by burning claws. A voidwaker also works." [3]
- Voidwaker: listed for "One-shotting melee frem", pair with a Lightbearer for spec regen [1][3]. Eldritch nightmare staff: same one-shot-melee-frem role [1]. runescapeguides: "SGS or Eldritch are both good, use them on the correct style Frem/Bees for a guaranteed Max Hit" [5].
- Defence-drain weapons: Sol "is not immune to stat reduction, so a dragon warhammer or elder maul can help make him easier to hit; however, for a first quiver, dragon claws or a similar special weapon are much more valuable to end the fight quickly once he goes below 10% HP." Both carry the wiki footnote "Sol Heredit only, not recommended for first quiver" [1].

### First attempts vs consistent clears
The wiki does not publish separate "first attempt" vs "consistent clear" inventories per style, but its GM time / High efficiency tabs show the experienced-player contrast: GM time carries only 3 Saradomin brew(4) + 8 Sanfew serum(4) + 1 Super combat, 1 Divine ranging, 1 Divine super combat, and High efficiency carries 1 Saradomin brew(4), 1 Guthix rest(4), 1 Surge potion, 7 Sanfew serum(4), 1 Divine super combat, 1 Divine ranging, both with far more switches (6-7-way ranged switch, Book of the dead offhand) [1]. "With some lazy flicking of offensive prayers, 7 super restores is a comfortable amount of prayer to have per KC" appears only in those experienced tabs [1]. runescapeguides supplies advice: "Adjust as necessary, you need at least 1 supercombat for Boss as you melee it", optional "Fally Shield 4, Heart is good but not necessary" [5].

## Prayer and restores (as it affects the loadout)

- Brew/restore counts are explicitly personal: "Saradomin brews and super restores; the quantity may differ from person to person, depending on how much you prayer flick" [1]. The concrete first-quiver examples carry 8-9 Super restore(4) and 5-6 Saradomin brew(4) (see above) [1].
- Blasphemy modifier: drains prayer points by 20% of damage taken (tier I), 40% (II), 60% (III). All three tiers are rated "Yes" picks: "This modifier is a drain on resources, but requires no conscious effort to deal with... The only way this kills you is if you habitually camp low prayer." Wave 1 always offers a selection of Relentless, Blasphemy and Frailty, so beginners routinely run Blasphemy and pay for it in restores [1].
- 1 Sanfew serum(4) is brought to cure venom when picking Mantimayhem II (a Noxious halberd special can substitute) [1].
- The Saradomin godsword special doubles as prayer restoration during waves [1][3].
- In the Sol fight, taking a Light Beam hit with Blasphemy active "will likely result in your prayer being smited" [1].
- Suggested Prayer level is 85+ [1].

## Beginner gear floor

- Wiki headline floor: "completions are possible with inexpensive gear, though at least a bow of Faerdhinen with full crystal armour along with an abyssal tentacle is recommended for a first quiver" [1]. The Budget tab repeats: "It is highly reccomended [sic] to obtain a bowfa setup for a first quiver", with scorching bow + Masori armour as next-best without bowfa and the hunters' sunlight crossbow rated worse [1].
- Melee floor: "a strong slashing melee weapon (i.e., abyssal tentacle or better) is essential" because of Sol's +750 magic and +825 ranged defence bonuses with Magic level 300 [1]. Sun fish: "An abyssal tentacle does the job just fine" for the boss, slash ladder scythe > soulreaper axe > noxious halberd (needs Myopia) > abyssal tentacle [3]. Osmumten's fang is the budget tab's primary wave melee weapon, beating noxious halberd on everything except Sol [1]. Sun fish stats the floor as "you should at least have a bofa setup and a fang, but a megarare will be very helpful" [3].
- Bloodmoon/Eclipse: Blood moon chestplate and Blood moon tassets appear only as bottom-tier body/legs alternatives (alongside Fighter torso/Bandos), and Eclipse atlatl appears only as the bottom-tier ranged weapon in the Ranged/Magic/Melee weapon3 rows. No wiki setup recommends full Bloodmoon or Eclipse sets [1].
- Tank gear: Justiciar does not appear in any wiki Colosseum table [1]. A Dinh's bulwark appears once, in the melee-only example inventory [1]. Sun fish addresses both directly: "none of the setups I showed contain them... Use defensive armour as a tool to help you learn, not as a crutch you use to avoid learning" [3]. runescapeguides (Feb 2025) does include a defensive ladder: "Ely -> Fortified Ward -> Unfortified Ward -> Crystal Shield -> Defender (assuming no scythe)" and "Guardian Boots -> Dragon Boots" [5].
- Megarare priority if buying one: Sun fish: "If you can only afford one of them, get the tbow. Trust me, you will get way more mileage out of it." [3] Kaoz (Apr 2024 meta): "prioritize the Scythe with an Ultor ring and then Tbow Masori because Torva doesn't really offer too much other than defense so leave it as your last upgrade" [4].
- Cost expectation: "About 500k per death at the high end, expect to use up to 100M including supplies" [5].
- runescapeguides full up/downgrade ladders (Feb 2025, verbatim): "Scythe -> Saeldor -> Tent Whip", "Shadow -> Tbow + a few range switches -> Bofa + crystal and a few switches", "Ancestral/Virtus -> Ahrims", "Torva -> Bandos/Faceguard or Setup", "Lightbearer -> Bring (just get an LB)", "Infernal Cape -> Fire cape (go do Inferno lol)", "Glove Switches can always be combined into Bgloves if desired" [5].

## Conflicts and unknowns

- Reddit r/2007scape threads: UNCONFIRMED. Reddit blocks both the search crawler and direct API access from this environment (HTTP block pages on www/old/api endpoints, search tool reports the domain inaccessible). No Reddit-sourced claims are included.
- Sun fish shows four concrete example setups on screen at 6:15 (max twisted bow, shadow, budget, low-death-fee twisted bow) [3]. The on-screen slot contents are UNCONFIRMED (not recoverable from captions). Spoken guidance is captured above.
- Kaoz shows a "minimum gear" screen at 2:05 [4]. On-screen contents UNCONFIRMED for the same reason.
- Boots conflict: runescapeguides (Feb 2025) recommends "Guardian Boots -> Dragon Boots" [5]. The current wiki ladders are Avernic treads (max) > Primordial boots > Aranea boots > Echo boots (Ranged) and Echo boots > Aranea boots > Dragon boots (Budget) [1]. The article predates Avernic treads (released 23 July 2025) [2], and guardian boots are the Echo boots base item [2].
- Shield conflict: runescapeguides lists Elidinis' ward tiers ("Fortified Ward -> Unfortified Ward") in its shield ladder [5]. No ward appears in any current wiki Colosseum table, which list Elysian spirit shield / Avernic defender then Crystal shield / Dragon defender [1].
- Mage armour conflict: runescapeguides gives "Ancestral/Virtus -> Ahrims" [5]. The current wiki magic tab bottoms out at Virtus with melee-hybrid bodies, no Ahrim's [1].
- Meta shift: Kaoz (Apr 2024) and runescapeguides (Feb 2025) predate Oathplate armour (May 2025), Eye of ayak, Confliction gauntlets and Avernic treads (July 2025) [2], so none of those items appear in them. - The wiki Ranged tab legs2 row pairs Oathplate legs with Crystal legs (not Masori chaps (f)), unlike body2 which pairs Oathplate chest with Masori body (f). Faithful to wikitext, reason not stated [1].
- "Echo boots" naming: despite sounding like a newer echo item, they are guardian boots enhanced with an echo crystal from the Colosseum itself, released 20 March 2024 [2].
- We Do Raids text guide: recommended by Sun fish as a text alternative [3], but wedoraids.com exposes no public Colosseum guide page (resources pages contain no Colosseum links, guide presumably Discord-internal). UNCONFIRMED.
- tonsofxp (Jul 2025) provides no slot-by-slot loadout, only "Use max hit setups (Shadow for Melee, Venator for Mage, SGS spec for Range)" and 95+/maxed stats [6].
