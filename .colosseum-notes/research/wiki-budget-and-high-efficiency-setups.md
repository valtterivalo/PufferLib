# Fortis Colosseum wiki gear setups (ground truth, user-pasted 2026-06-16)

Source: https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Fortis_Colosseum/Strategies (wiki blocks
direct web fetch, so the user pasted the Equipment section). Mapping directed by
the user:

- wiki "Budget" tab        -> our BUDGET kit (currently COLO_*_BEGINNER_* / profile BEGINNER)
- wiki "High efficiency" tab -> our SPEEDRUN kit (currently COLO_*_SPEEDRUN_*)

Thrall lifetime per kit (user): budget = 99 ticks (no Master-CA doubling),
high-efficiency = 198 ticks (Master-CA x2). Thralls are full-fidelity: ignore
accuracy, static max hit by tier, net a static DPS improvement. Both kits run a
rune pouch for Death Charge (+ thralls).

General note from the wiki: a strong slashing melee weapon is essential for Sol
(high ranged/magic def); waves clear well with ranged or magic; at least one
ranged/magic option is strongly recommended. Tumeken's shadow one-shots the melee
frem; SGS one-shots the ranged frem; claws finish Sol < 10% and NPCs before
reinforcements.
