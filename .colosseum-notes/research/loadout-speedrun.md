## Speed thresholds

- "Colosseum Speed-Runner" is a grandmaster combat achievement: "Complete the Colosseum with a total time of 24:00 or less." Released 17 April 2024 (update "Undead Pirates Tweaks, Varlamore CAs & More"), task id 550. It was renamed from "Colosseum Speed-Chaser" on 24 April 2024 [2].
- "Colosseum Speed-Chaser" is the master-tier version: "Complete the Colosseum with a total time of 28:00 or less." Same release date, task id 549, renamed from "Colosseum Speed-Trialist" on 24 April 2024 [3].
- The wiki on the GM threshold: "Compared to the other Grandmaster speedrun times, this is an extremely lenient time to achieve. Gear does not matter very much, as any megarare setup or even an Osmumten's fang and venator bow in Bandos-tier gear can clear this threshold comfortably." [2]
- World record context: "As of 1 June 2026, the global best glory achieved is 60,411, and the best time is 13:55.20" (in-game global scoreboard, per wiki) [4]. Holder of the 13:55.20: UNCONFIRMED (not named in any consulted source).
- Record progression: 18:57.6 ("first s19 on the board", aatykon, 2024-03-24) [9]; 17:32 (Gnomonkey, 2024-03-30) [6]; 16:10 (Gnomonkey, 2024-04-01, who notes a competing record by "Ausah" "over a minute faster than mine" just prior) [7]; 14:48 labelled "current Fortis Colosseum world record as of time of this post" (DeerDeerDeer, 2025-05-19) [8]. Gnomonkey's April 2024 claim that "a sub 15 is effectively not possible without some new knowledge or patches" [7] was overtaken by the 13:55.20 on the 2026 scoreboard [4].

## Combat styles used

Speedrun setups are hybrid, melee-primary with ranged and magic switches:

- Melee camp: "Scythe of vitur" is the speedrun tab's only listed melee weapon [1]. Sol Heredit context: "Sol is weak to slash, making the scythe of Vitur the strongest option; a soulreaper axe, blade of Saeldor, or abyssal tentacle are acceptable alternatives" [1]. Record-runner view (March 2024): "Scythe is a MUST. Even just using a Salad blade is 30% dps loss compared to Scythe." [5] Soulreaper axe appears only in the learner tabs' second-tier weapon rows, never in the GM time / High efficiency tabs [1].
- Ranged: "The venator bow should be used whenever triple bounces are possible - mainly on waves 1-6, and on 10-11. Scything large NPCs is the next-best DPS, though the twisted bow is still useful for tick-filling and tight situations." [1] Gnomonkey (17:32 WR): "I use NE pillar to get the perfect Venator Bow setups 1-6, then I go back south and melee camp with Scythe afterwards. Venator Bow maxes a 91 in this setup (with ring), which outdps' Scythe." [6]
- Magic: a Tumeken's shadow switch ("3-way" in GM time wording) used to one-shot the melee Fremennik warbander; thralls and Death Charge are run off the Arceuus spellbook ("Thralls and Death Charge" with a rune pouch of fire/aether/death/blood runes) [1]. Gnomonkey: "I think Ancients isn't the play, thralls provide so much dps. Everything in Colosseum has high mage defense." [5]

## Spec weapons and usage

- Speedrun spec set (High efficiency): "Burning claws / Dragon claws + Elder maul / Dragon warhammer" [1]. GM time variant: "Burning claws / Dragon claws + Dragon warhammer / Elder maul", with footnote that the elder maul "Requires one more inventory space, as you can no longer offhand the book of the dead" (maul is two-handed) [1].
- Claws choice: "The difference between burning claws and dragon claws is very minimal for most players. To use burning claws effectively, you have to avoid staying on 100% special attack energy, and do your best to get the maximum burn time on NPCs." [1]
- When claws are used: "Dumping dragon claws/burning claws special attacks is a very effective way to finish the Sol fight once he goes below 10% HP. Claws are also very useful for finishing NPCs before reinforcements spawn." [1] Gnomonkey: "I took Claws to help me spec mobs down faster" [5]; "I use claws to nuke the Serpent Mage for skips" [6].
- Hammers are for Sol: "He is not immune to stat reduction, so a dragon warhammer or elder maul can help make him easier to hit" [1]. Gnomonkey: "Dragon Warhammer is 7s each time save, and better than double clawing the boss" [6]; on his 16:10, the boss wave "could've been about 10 seconds faster with both hammers" [7]. The GM time concrete image starts the run with Dragon warhammer + Book of the dead equipped [1].
- Saradomin godsword: in the GM time inventory ("Saradomin godsword to one-shot ranged frem" in learner tabs); in High efficiency it is optional — "Instead of the 4-tick weapon, you can still opt to bring a Saradomin godsword, or simply scythe the ranged frem in max melee. This will require you to free up one more inventory slot." [1]
- Voidwaker: listed only in learner/budget special rows, footnoted "One-shotting melee frem", and "using special attacks on the shaman can kill it quickly" (claws/burning claws/voidwaker) — not in either speedrun tab [1].
- Eldritch nightmare staff: learner tabs only, footnoted for one-shotting the melee frem [1].
- Tonalztics of ralos: not mentioned anywhere on the strategies page (the only "Ralos" hit is Sol's quote "Ralos guides my hand!") [1].
- Dinh's bulwark: appears in the learner melee-only inventory [1]; Gnomonkey on records: "I tried Bulwark and the line of sight issues still need to be fleshed out a lot more before it beats out just clawing targets" [6].

## Modifier route effects on supplies

No consulted source documents a fixed "standard speedrun modifier route" — UNCONFIRMED. What the wiki documents:

- Wave 1 always offers "Relentless, Blasphemy and Frailty"; "Red Flag and Dynamic Duo become available before wave 7"; later offers prefer upgrades of previously picked tiers; "Picking multiple, manageable T1 modifiers as soon as possible will reduce the chance of being forced to pick an undesirable modifier in the later waves." The recommendation table "assume[s] that the player is learning for their first quiver" [1].
- Supply interactions stated by the wiki: Mantimayhem (II) makes manticores venomous — "The venom from (II) is a non-issue if you bring Sanfew serums or a Noxious halberd and use the special attack to cure it"; both speedrun inventories carry 7-8 Sanfew serums [1]. Blasphemy drains prayer by 20/40/60% of damage taken — "a drain on resources, but requires no conscious effort" [1]. Relentless increases damage taken and "mak[es] tank armour less useful... has less impact on players good at praying against enemy attacks, or in lower-defence armour" [1].
- Recommendation column (general, learner-framed): Yes — Blasphemy (all tiers), Doom (I), Frailty (I, II), Mantimayhem (II), Myopia (I, II), Relentless (I), Reentry (III), Volatility (III); No — Bees! (all), Doom (III), Dynamic Duo, Frailty (III), Mantimayhem (III), Red Flag, Quartet, Totemic, Reentry (II); Maybe — the rest [1].
- Wave 12 specifics: Volatility "is an easy pick at wave 12, since it only does damage after Sol is already dead"; Reentry 2/3 users "should avoid splattering the centre with molten sand" before Sol; Quartet spawns an extra frem at the Sol fight start [1].
- Myopia is noted as the reason halberd users struggle at Sol if not taken ("A noxious halberd... is very difficult to use if Myopia was not taken or offered during the waves") [1].
- Record-era state (March 2024, dated): "over 80% of them make you lose a run automatically, or reset spam" (Gnomonkey on the modifier system pre-rework) [5].
